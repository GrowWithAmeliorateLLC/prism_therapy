What it is

Whole-body care that starts in the mouth.

Orofacial myofunctional disorders (OMDs) affect the muscles and structures we use to talk, eat, and sleep. Left unaddressed, they can influence dental alignment, breathing, and speech at any age. Our integrative approach looks for the root cause — not just the symptom — and gently re-educates those muscles so the whole system works together.

Myo is across the lifespan

Myo is for every age. We're the only clinic in the Austin area trained in infant myofunctional therapy, and we also specialize in seeing toddlers for myo, feeding, and speech — plus teens and adults, including pre- and post-tongue-tie release.

Signs it might help

Does any of this sound familiar?

✓Mouth breathing or snoring
✓A lisp or ongoing speech-sound errors
✓Picky or messy eating; long, drawn-out meals
✓Dental misalignment or an open bite
✓A low resting tongue posture
✓A tongue-thrust swallow
✓Thumb sucking past toddlerhood
✓Drooling beyond the expected age
✓Restless sleep or grinding

Good to know

Myofunctional therapy FAQ

How long does orofacial myofunctional therapy take?
Most people notice changes after about 12 weeks of therapy, though the exact timeline depends on the individual and their goals.
When can children begin?
Children can begin as early as infancy through toddlerhood and beyond, with properly trained speech and occupational therapists. We're the only clinic in the Austin area trained in infant myofunctional therapy.
Can we do it at home?
Yes. A big part of success is short, simple exercises you practice at home between sessions, so the new patterns become automatic.
Do you help before and after a tongue-tie release?
We do. Pre- and post-frenectomy therapy helps prepare the muscles and supports recovery so the release leads to lasting, functional change.
