Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with adults, or only children?
Both. We treat every age — from a baby's first latch to adults — with speech, myofunctional, and airway support.
When should my child start therapy?
As soon as you have a concern. Many children begin as early as infancy when struggling with tongue ties and/or feeding concerns.
Do you take insurance?
We're a private-pay clinic, which lets us give you focused, unhurried care. We provide superbills so you can seek reimbursement from your insurance.
What is orofacial myofunctional therapy?
It retrains the muscles of the face, mouth, and tongue to support proper breathing, sleep, swallowing, and speech. Children can begin as early as infancy through toddlerhood and beyond with properly trained speech and occupational therapists.
How do I get started?
Two ways: if you already know you want an evaluation, you can book one directly online. If you're not sure, use the "Not sure where to start?" form and we'll point you to the right first step.
Where are you located?
Two Austin-area locations: Harmony Collaborative South (South Austin) and Harmony Collaborative Round Rock.
