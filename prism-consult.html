Good to know

Common first questions

Do I need to know what's wrong before reaching out?
Not at all. "I'm not sure, but something feels off" is a perfectly good reason to reach out — helping you sort that out is part of what we do.
What happens after I submit the form?
We'll email you new patient information and answers to common questions, and point you toward the right first step. When you're ready, you can book an evaluation.
Do you take insurance?
We're a private-pay clinic, which lets us give focused, unhurried care. We provide superbills so you can seek reimbursement from your insurance.
What if I already know I want an evaluation?
Wonderful — you can skip this form and book your evaluation directly.
