What it is

Making mealtimes feel safe again.

Some children struggle with the many steps of eating — for sensory, oral-motor, or behavioral reasons. We meet your child where they are and, using proven, low-pressure methods, gently expand the foods and textures they can manage, so meals become calmer and more nourishing for the whole family.

No pressure, ever

Our approach is playful and child-led — we build trust with food first, so progress feels safe rather than scary.

Signs it might help

Does any of this sound familiar?

✓A very limited list of "safe" foods
✓Refusing anything new or unfamiliar
✓Doesn't chew food thoroughly
✓Gagging or choking at meals
✓Feeling sick after eating
✓Strong reactions to textures
✓Meltdowns or stress at mealtime
✓Trouble transitioning to solids
✓Mealtimes that drag on and on

Good to know

Feeding therapy FAQ

Does feeding therapy help picky eaters?
Yes — we help with texture aversions, a limited food list, and swallowing difficulties, using gentle, child-led approaches.
How will I know it's working?
Signs include accepting new foods, healthier weight, and more typical chewing and swallowing. Some families see change in as little as two weeks.
Can we practice at home?
Yes — simple things like attractive meal presentation and letting your child help prepare food make a real difference.
