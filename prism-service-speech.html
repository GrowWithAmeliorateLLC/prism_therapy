What it is

Meeting each person right where they are.

Sometimes finding your words is hard — and it can happen for all kinds of reasons. Our speech-language pathologists evaluate what's really going on, then blend proven therapy with play and everyday activities so learning feels natural. We meet your child at their level and grow with them, and we work with adults across the lifespan too.

Kids learn through play

For little ones, we weave therapy into games and activities so sessions feel like fun — while the skills underneath are anything but random.

Good to know

Speech therapy FAQ

Do you work with adults, or only children?
Both. We provide speech therapy across the lifespan — toddlers, kids, teens, and adults.
Is it normal for a toddler not to speak clearly?
It's normal up to about two to three years old, though you should begin to understand your toddler's vocalizations around year two. If something feels off, reaching out is a great first step.
When should my child begin speech therapy?
As soon as you have concerns and have talked with your pediatrician. Many children begin as early as one year old.
What's the difference between a speech therapist and a speech-language pathologist?
There's no difference besides the title — both help people manage and overcome issues with speaking, swallowing, and chewing.
