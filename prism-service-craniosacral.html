What it is

Two related modalities, one gentle goal.

We offer two related approaches — craniosacral therapy and craniosacral fascial therapy (CFT). Both are light-touch, hands-on modalities that ease restrictions in the craniosacral and fascial systems — the connective tissue that links the whole body. They're calming rather than forceful, which is why they work beautifully for everyone from newborns to adults, and they pair naturally with our feeding and myofunctional care.

A whole-body fit

Because fascia connects everything, this work often supports sleep, digestion, and comfort alongside our other therapies.

Good to know

Craniosacral therapy FAQ

Is craniosacral fascial therapy safe?
It's gentle and safe for most people. It is not recommended for anyone with a stroke, aneurysm, or active brain bleed — we'll always review history first.
How many sessions will we need?
It varies by person and goal. Your therapist will talk through a suggested plan after the evaluation.
What are the benefits?
Improved nerve function and faster physical and mental healing, along with support for sleep, digestion, and tension relief.
